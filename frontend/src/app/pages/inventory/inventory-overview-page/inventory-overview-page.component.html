Tier 1

Ingredient Category

Macro classification encapsulating nutritional & culinary categories. Serves as top-level taxonomy.

Examples: Protein & Dairy, Fiber & Produce, Carbs & Grains
Tier 2

Ingredient Group

Sub-classification organizing related culinary ingredients under a parent Category.

Examples: Meat, Seafood, Beans, Vegetables, Fruits, Grains
Tier 3

Ingredient

Master catalog culinary definition. Shared by recipes and physical inventory items.

Examples: Chicken Breast, Gala Apple, Jasmine Rice, Olive Oil
Tier 4

Ingredient Item

Physical stock instance sitting in your fridge/pantry with specific quantity, expiration, and location.

Examples: 2 lbs Chicken Breast exp. Jul 30 in Refrigerator
